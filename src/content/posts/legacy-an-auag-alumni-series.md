On Friday, September 25, 2026, AUAG opened Homecoming Weekend with the first
edition of **Legacy: An AUAG Alumni Series** at the Howard Performing Arts
Center.

## What happened

Alumni from law, engineering, medicine, and capital joined students for a
moderated panel on the decisions that shaped their careers — the turns they
took, the ones they passed on, and what they would tell a student standing
where they once stood.

The conversation ran from 2:30 to 5:00 PM, followed by open networking with the
panelists and other alumni in the room.

## Watch the recording

The full session was recorded. [Watch it on
YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lQ8JFB2IuWc).

## What comes next

Legacy is a series, not a one-off. Future sessions will bring different
alumni and different fields to the same room. Students and alumni who want to
be part of the next one can [join the network](https://join.auactiongroup.com/alumni).
