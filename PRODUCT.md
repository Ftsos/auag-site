# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Andrews University alumni (mid-career professionals at companies like Duke Health,
Clifford Chance, Bank of America — deciding whether to give time to their alma
mater) and current Andrews students (deciding whether AUAG can actually open
doors for them). Secondary: university staff and faculty who vouch for AUAG
internally. Visitors arrive from Instagram, email signatures, and word of mouth;
the site's job is to make joining feel like joining something established.

## Product Purpose

The public face of the Andrews University Action Group — a student organization
that turns the Andrews alumni network into careers (mentorship, internships,
ventures). The site must convert two audiences (alumni sign-up, student
application), announce the Legacy alumni series event, and prove credibility
with real outcomes and real companies. Success = an alumnus who lands here
believes this org is worth two hours a month.

## Brand Personality

Trusted elder network. Confident, institutional-but-not-stuffy, warm toward
community, serious about outcomes. "Accelerating opportunity" — action over
aspiration. Premium and established rather than scrappy; personable rather
than corporate.

## Anti-references

- SaaS startup landing pages: hype copy, gradient accents, feature grids,
  hero-metric counters.
- Generic university-department pages: clip-art warmth, bulletin-board layout.
- The site's own former self: dark-mode tech-minimalist with glow effects.
- Anything that reads "AI-generated template": eyebrow labels on every section,
  identical icon-card grids, lorem-flavored copy.

## Design Principles

1. **The Frame is the brand.** Black chrome is structure (nav, frame, footer,
   one featured moment); the warm-white canvas is where life happens. Shared
   visual family with the internal AUAG OS.
2. **Proof over promise.** Real names, real companies, real dates — presented
   like a record, not a pitch. Never invent people, stats, or partnerships.
3. **Red is a scalpel.** One chromatic accent, reserved for action and emphasis.
4. **Editorial, not templated.** Rows, rules, and asymmetry over identical card
   grids; typography carries the personality (Arimo display = the logo's voice,
   Space Grotesk = the numeric accent voice).
5. **Personable precision.** The org connects humans; the site should feel
   composed but never cold — human copy, generous whitespace, real photography
   when it exists.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: ≥4.5:1 body contrast, visible focus (red focus ring system-wide),
full keyboard reachability (menus, overlays inert-managed), reduced-motion
alternatives for every animation, no hover-only information on touch devices.
